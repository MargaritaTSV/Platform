One of Galileo Galilei's ``telescopic'' discoveries was the phases of Venus,
which resemble the phases of the Moon. Is it possible to see Mars from Earth
as a thin crescent, with the naked eye or at least in a telescope? And Earth
from Mars? Explain your answer.
